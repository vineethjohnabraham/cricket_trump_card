Computer skill

🃏 Everyone gets 25 cards  •  Collect ALL the cards to be champion

How to play

1

Everyone gets 25 cricket star cards.

2

On your turn, tap the stat on your top card you think is the biggest.

3

Everyone flips. The highest number takes all the cards.

4

A tie? The cards go into the pot for the next round.

5

No cards left? You're out. Collect every card to be champion! 🏆

💡 Tip: Bowlers have lots of WICKETS. Batters have lots of RUNS.
